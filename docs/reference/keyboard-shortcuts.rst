Keyboard Shortcuts
==================

Shortcuts work in the Library tab, both in the timeline and in the loupe.
They are ignored while typing in a text field. On macOS use ⌘ where Ctrl
is listed.

Culling
-------

Culling keys apply to the photo shown in the loupe or, in the timeline,
to every selected photo.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Key
     - Action
   * - P
     - Pick
   * - X
     - Reject
   * - U
     - Unflag (neither pick nor reject)
   * - 1 – 5
     - Rate one to five stars
   * - 0
     - Remove the rating
   * - B
     - Make the photo the best of its series; again to withdraw the choice
       (:ref:`features-series`)
   * - Ctrl+Z
     - Undo the last change
   * - Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y
     - Redo

Timeline
--------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 70

   * - Key
     - Action
   * - ← → ↑ ↓
     - Move to the neighbouring photo. Up and down keep the column, across
       day headers.
   * - Shift + arrow
     - Extend the selection
   * - Home / End
     - First / last photo
   * - Enter
     - Open the selected photo in the loupe
   * - Esc
     - Clear the selection
   * - /
     - Search photos by description (:ref:`features-ai-search`)
   * - C
     - Compare the selected photos, or the selected photo's series
       (:ref:`features-compare`)
   * - S
     - Split the series before the selected photo; again to undo
   * - J
     - Join the selected photo to the series of the photo taken before it;
       again to undo
